Safety before scale

Data, Storage, and Privacy

The moment a classroom tool stores student information, the project is no longer just a clever prototype. It becomes a data-governance question.

Lowest risk

No stored student data

Static pages, calculators, flashcards, simulations, reflection prompts, and tools that reset when the page closes.

Medium risk

Local-only storage

`localStorage` saves on one device/browser. Useful for demos, but not a real roster system or secure student record.

Higher risk

Cloud accounts or databases

Anything with logins, rosters, names, grades, uploads, analytics, or shared records needs district review.

Beginner rule

Do not collect what you do not need.

Avoid names when possible

Use initials, demo data, pseudonyms, or no identifiers at all.

Avoid grades and sensitive notes

Do not use a vibe-coded prototype as an official gradebook, behavior log, intervention record, or IEP/504 workspace.

Assume public hosting is public

GitHub Pages is excellent for static resources. It is not private student-data infrastructure.

Ask before adding accounts

Authentication, databases, and cloud storage should go through approved district systems.

A safe first prototype helps people see the idea. It does not have to become the production system.
