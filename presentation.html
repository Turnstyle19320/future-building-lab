Systemic draft

Three radical decisions from 2026

  • "Vines over Walls" Act: dissolve seat-time requirements.
  • Decoupling "Care" and "Clericals": automate non-human tasks.
  • Radical Transparency Compact: shared success ledger.
The correction
Facilitator prompt

Feasibility changed the direction.

None of these things are within the power of an individual teacher at the moment.

We need actions that are bold and scalable, but within their power to do.

That is the hinge.

Not "What should the system do someday?" but "What can an educator prototype now?"
